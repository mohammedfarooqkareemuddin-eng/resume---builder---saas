About ResumeRocket

From zero coding experience to building a free resume builder for the world – in just 7 months.

Our Story

ResumeRocket was born from a simple frustration: why do job seekers have to pay expensive subscription fees just to create a professional, ATS-friendly resume?

I am a solo developer based in Hyderabad, India. Less than a year ago, I had zero coding experience. I used free AI tools, sheer determination, and countless late nights to learn web development, build this platform, and launch it to the public.

Today, ResumeRocket provides a fully functional, professional resume-building tool that helps job seekers globally structure their experience and download clean, formatted documents – completely free.

Our Mission

To provide 100% free, professional career tools to every job seeker. No hidden charges, no forced account creation, and no unnecessary data collection. Just a reliable tool that helps you get hired.

Why Choose ResumeRocket?

  • 100% Free – No credit card, no subscription, no trial periods.
  • No Sign-Up Required – Just open the site and start building your resume instantly.
  • Structured Formatting – Templates are designed with clean, standard layouts to support compatibility with common Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
  • Privacy First – Your data is processed locally in your browser and is never sent to our servers.
  • Made in India – Built with dedication from Hyderabad.

Our Vision

We believe that professional career tools should not be a privilege. Everyone deserves access to quality resources, regardless of their budget. We are building a future where free, professional tools are the standard.

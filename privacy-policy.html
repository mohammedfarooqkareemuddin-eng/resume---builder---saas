Privacy Policy – ResumeRocket

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ResumeRocket (resumerocket.tech). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our free resume building service.

2. Information We Collect

We do not store your resume data on our servers. ResumeRocket is designed with your privacy in mind. All resume creation happens directly in your browser. The personal information you enter (name, email, work experience) is processed locally on your device and is never sent to or stored by us.

However, we do use third-party cookies for analytics and advertising purposes (see Section 4).

3. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect or store your resume data, we do not use it for any purpose. Your resume data stays on your device and is never shared with anyone.

4. Third-Party Services and Cookies

We use the following third-party services to improve our website and monetize our free tool:

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our site. It collects anonymous data such as page views, time spent, and general location (country level). It does not collect personal information like your name or resume content.
  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy

5. Your Rights

You have the right to know what data we collect. Since we do not store your personal resume data, there is no data to access, correct, or delete on our servers. For cookie data collected by Google, you can manage your preferences via Google Ads Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

ResumeRocket does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Our service is intended for job seekers of all ages, but we do not target children specifically.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

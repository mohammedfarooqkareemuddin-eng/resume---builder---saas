The Top 10 Resume Mistakes That Cost You Interviews

📅 September 24, 2026 | ⏱️ 10 min read | ✍️ ResumeRocket Team

You've applied to dozens of jobs. You have the qualifications, the experience, and the skills. Yet, the interview calls never come.

What's going wrong?

Often, it's not your abilities that are holding you back—it's your resume. Even highly qualified candidates get rejected because of simple, avoidable mistakes.

This guide covers the top 10 resume mistakes that cost job seekers interviews, and exactly how to fix them.

Small mistakes can cost you big opportunities

1. Typos and Grammatical Errors

The Mistake: A single typo can make your resume look careless. Recruiters often interpret spelling errors as a lack of attention to detail.

How to Fix It:

  • Proofread your resume multiple times.
  • Use tools like Grammarly or Microsoft Word's spell check.
  • Read your resume aloud to catch errors.
  • Ask a friend or family member to review it.

2. Using a Generic Resume for Every Job

The Mistake: Sending the same resume to 50 different jobs is one of the biggest reasons candidates don't get interviews.

How to Fix It:

  • Customize your resume for each application.
  • Use keywords from the job description.
  • Adjust your professional summary to match the role.
  • Highlight the most relevant experience and skills.

3. Missing or Incorrect Contact Information

The Mistake: It sounds obvious, but many candidates forget to include their email, phone number, or LinkedIn profile—or they include outdated information.

How to Fix It:

  • Double-check your contact details.
  • Use a professional email address (firstname.lastname@gmail.com).
  • Include your LinkedIn profile URL.
  • Make sure your phone number is current.

Missing contact details can disqualify you instantly

4. Fake or Exaggerated Information

The Mistake: Inflating your experience, skills, or achievements might get you an interview—but it will destroy your credibility when the truth comes out.

How to Fix It:

  • Be honest about your experience.
  • Focus on real achievements and quantify them.
  • Use accurate job titles and dates.
  • Never claim skills you don't have.

5. Irrelevant Work Experience

The Mistake: Listing every job you've ever had—especially ones unrelated to the role you're applying for—clutters your resume.

How to Fix It:

  • Focus on the most relevant experience.
  • Keep older or unrelated roles brief.
  • Highlight transferable skills.
  • Use a functional or hybrid format if needed.

6. Broken Links or Wrong Email Addresses

The Mistake: You include a link to your portfolio or LinkedIn profile—but it doesn't work. Or worse, your email address has a typo, so recruiters can't reach you.

How to Fix It:

  • Test every link on your resume.
  • Double-check your email address.
  • Use a URL shortener if links are too long.
  • Update outdated portfolio links.

Broken links or wrong email can cost you the opportunity

7. Long Paragraphs (Too Much Text)

The Mistake: Recruiters spend only 6-7 seconds scanning your resume. If your resume is filled with long paragraphs, they won't read it.

How to Fix It:

  • Use bullet points instead of paragraphs.
  • Keep each bullet to 1-2 lines.
  • Focus on achievements, not duties.
  • Use white space to make your resume scannable.

8. Poor Quality Photo or Unclear Design

The Mistake: A low-quality, unprofessional photo can hurt your chances. Similarly, an over-designed resume with too many colors and graphics can be distracting.

How to Fix It:

  • Use a professional headshot if including a photo.
  • Keep your design clean and simple.
  • Avoid excessive colors and graphics.
  • Prioritize readability over visual complexity.

9. Not Updating Old Information

The Mistake: Your resume still lists your old phone number, outdated skills, or a job you left five years ago.

How to Fix It:

  • Review your resume every 3-6 months.
  • Update your skills and certifications.
  • Remove outdated information.
  • Keep your resume current with your career progress.

Resume checklist before you apply

10. Ignoring Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)

The Mistake: Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes before a human sees them. If your resume isn't formatted correctly, it gets rejected automatically.

How to Fix It:

  • Use standard fonts and formatting.
  • Avoid tables, graphics, and columns.
  • Use keywords from the job description.
  • Submit your resume as a PDF or DOCX file.

Make your resume easy to read for both machines and humans

Quick Resume Checklist Before You Apply

  • ☐ No typos or grammatical errors
  • ☐ Contact information is correct
  • ☐ Resume is tailored to the job
  • ☐ Achievements are quantified
  • ☐ All links work
  • ☐ Formatting is clean and readable
  • ☐ No outdated information
  • ☐ ATS-friendly format
  • ☐ File is named professionally

Final Thoughts

Your resume is your first impression. Avoid these 10 common mistakes, and you'll dramatically increase your chances of landing interviews.

Remember: Small improvements lead to big opportunities.
